Meet the app

PhotoTend

Reclaim storage. Keep the memories.

PhotoTend helps you find exactly what's eating your storage — duplicate photos, big videos, endless screenshots — so you can free up space without losing what matters.

Find Duplicate Photos

Spot exact duplicate photos and review them in simple groups — keep what you want, delete the rest, all with your confirmation.

Compare Similar Shots

Group photos from the same moment and easily compare bursts or repeated shots — PhotoTend can even suggest the best one.

Review Blurry Photos

Browse potentially blurry photos by severity and view them at full size before deciding what stays in your library.

Organize Screenshots

Turn a messy screenshot pile into focused categories — chats, webpages, documents, and more — ready for quick review.

Clean Up Large Videos

Find the videos taking up the most space — 4K clips, screen recordings, slow-motion, and more — and decide what to keep.
